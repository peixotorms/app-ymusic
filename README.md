# YMusic

A music player for Android, Windows, and Linux. Stream from YouTube and YouTube Music, play local files, and download music for offline listening.

This is a personal fork of [YMusic V4](https://github.com/ymusic-io/YMusicV4), based on version 4.0.16.

## Features

- Browse YouTube Music, playlists, and your signed-in library.
- Like or unlike tracks from the phone or Android Auto.
- Control playback through Android Auto and cast audio to Google Cast speakers.
- Skip sponsored segments with SponsorBlock.
- Adjust audio buffering and manage offline downloads.
- Open a reorganized YouTube Music Home with favorites and mixes first.

Automatic crash reporting and anonymous usage statistics are removed.

Download builds from [Releases](https://github.com/peixotorms/app-ymusic/releases).

See the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) for dated changes, [build instructions](BUILDING.md) for development, and the [fork maintenance guide](UPSTREAM_MERGING.md) before merging upstream updates.
